How MoneyPrinter Works
A fully autonomous algorithmic trading system built on 30+ technical indicators, Claude AI second-opinion validation, and Alpaca paper execution — all running on free GitHub Actions cron schedules with zero servers required.
30+ Indicators 7 Strategies Claude AI Filter Alpaca Paper Trading 150+ Universe 4 Daily Sessions
Daily Pipeline
Stage 01
🔭
Discovery
08:30 ET
Screens 150+ liquid large/mid-caps for active movers. Criteria: market cap ≥ $10B, avg vol ≥ 2M, vol ratio ≥ 1.5×, price move ≥ 1.5% or within 3% of 52-week high. Top 10 promoted to watchlist. Claude rejects candidates without a clear business catalyst.
Stage 02
📊
Pre-Market Score
09:00 ET
Fetches live snapshots and 90 days of bars for every watchlist ticker. Computes 30+ indicators across trend, momentum, volatility, volume, and news categories. Bull/bear point system produces Net Score and Confidence. Claude AI then validates every decision.
Stage 03
⚡
Trading Day
09:30–16:00 ET
Executes buy/short/sell orders via Alpaca Paper API. Re-scans open positions every session for rescore. Applies macro filter (VIX + SPY regime) before each order. Risk controls enforce 2% base risk, 10% max position, 60% max exposure, and a kill switch at −3% daily P&L.
Stage 04
🌙
EOD Summary
16:15 ET
Fetches final portfolio snapshot from Alpaca. Computes daily P&L, win rate, and profit factor for closed trades. Appends daily history to live_feed.json on GitHub. Logs all decisions and trade outcomes for the dashboard.

Trading Strategies
📈
Trend Follow
trend_follow
Full EMA bull stack with strong directional momentum. Requires all three short-term EMAs aligned, ADX confirming trend strength, positive MACD histogram, and volume confirmation — a classic swing setup.
SL 2.0× ATR TP 2.5R Swing 2–5d
Entry Conditions
EMA9 > EMA21 > EMA50 ADX > 18 MACD hist > 0 Volume confirm
Priority 4 in classification hierarchy
↩️
Mean Reversion
mean_reversion
Buys oversold dips or shorts overbought spikes when RSI or Bollinger %B reaches an extreme. Entry is only taken if price is still above the 50-day SMA — preventing falling-knife trades. Target is the 20-day SMA (Bollinger middle), not a fixed R multiple.
SL 1.5× ATR TP 2.0R / SMA20 Scalp 0–2d
Entry Conditions
RSI < 38 or > 68 BB %B < 0.15 or > 0.85 Price > SMA50 No full EMA alignment
Priority 5 in classification hierarchy
🚀
Breakout
breakout
Momentum play on a clean technical breakout above R1 pivot or 52-week high, confirmed by elevated volume. Highest risk:reward target of all strategies since breakouts often extend. Entry within 2% above the level to avoid chasing.
SL 1.5× ATR TP 3.0R Swing 1–3d
Entry Conditions
Price > R1 pivot or 52wk high Within 2% of breakout level Volume ratio > 1.3×
Priority 2 in classification hierarchy
📉
Breakdown
breakdown
Short-side momentum trade when price breaks below the S1 support pivot with volume confirmation, signaling institutional distribution. Mirror image of the breakout strategy but for bearish setups.
SL 1.5× ATR TP 2.5R Swing 1–3d
Entry Conditions
Price breaks below S1 pivot Volume confirmation Short / Sell signal
Priority 3 in classification hierarchy
💥
Squeeze Breakout
squeeze_breakout
Highest-conviction setup: Bollinger Bands contract inside Keltner Channels (a "squeeze"), then price explodes through the Keltner upper band with two consecutive confirmed closes. Only valid on low-volatility names (ATR/price < 3%). Stop placed at the 10-day consolidation low.
SL 10d low TP 2.5R×dist Swing 2–4d
Entry Conditions
BB inside Keltner (squeeze) KC upper breakout 2 consecutive closes above KC ATR/price < 3%
Priority 1 — checked first in classification
📡
News Momentum
news_momentum
Catalyst-driven scalp triggered when strong positive or negative news sentiment combines with at least partial EMA alignment or a volume surge. Designed for same-day reactions to earnings, guidance, or macro announcements. Hype detection penalty still applies.
SL 1.5× ATR TP 2.0R Scalp same-day
Entry Conditions
News positive / negative signal EMA partial alignment OR volume surge
Priority 6 in classification hierarchy
🔀
Mixed
mixed
Fallback when no dominant pattern is identifiable. Confidence is automatically reduced by a 5% penalty to discourage marginal trades. Most mixed-strategy tickers result in a HOLD decision and are not traded. Treated as a signal to wait for a cleaner setup.
SL 2.0× ATR TP 2.0R Short hold
Characteristics
No dominant pattern −5% confidence penalty Usually → HOLD
Priority 7 — last resort fallback

Action thresholds: Net Score ≥ 65 + Confidence ≥ 0.65 → BUY  ·  Net ≥ 70 + Confidence ≥ 0.70 → SHORT  ·  Otherwise → HOLD. Net Score = Bull Points − Bear Points. Confidence = Net Score ÷ 100.
Claude AI Layer
🤖
Claude Sonnet — Second-Opinion Analyst
Every scored ticker (buy, hold, or short) is reviewed by Claude before execution. Claude is not a rubber-stamp — it can confirm, upgrade, or reject any signal the rules engine produces. Falls back silently if the API is unavailable, leaving the scorer's decision unchanged.
What Claude evaluates
For BUY candidates: Is there a plausible fundamental reason the stock should recover or continue higher? Is the dip caused by structural problems or sector decline? Is momentum actually turning, or is this a falling knife?
For SHORT candidates: Is there a genuine reason the stock should fall? Or is it strong for a real reason (earnings growth, market leadership)? Is the setup confirmed or is it just high RSI?
Inputs provided: 30+ indicator values, bull/bear signals triggered, multi-timeframe velocity returns, news headlines and sentiment, current held position context.
Discovery filter: Claude also runs a pre-screen on discovery candidates to reject tickers without a clear business catalyst driving the volume/price activity.
Execution thresholds
Auto-Execute Net ≥ 85 & Conf ≥ 0.85
Claude Runs Net ≥ 60 & Conf ≥ 0.65
Macro: VIX Halt VIX > 35 → no new longs
Macro: SPY Bear Bull signals −20% to −50%
Hype detection
Penalties: Cramer mentions, Reddit/WSB hype, FOMO language, short-squeeze narratives.
Boosts: Earnings beats, raised guidance, insider buying, analyst upgrades with price targets.
Velocity penalty: Large recent gains reduce confidence. Halved for fundamental breakouts, doubled for hype. Hard cap at −0.45.
